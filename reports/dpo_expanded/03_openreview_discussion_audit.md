## HyPO — Mitigating Mismatch within Reference-based Preference Optimization

[Forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=k79Un1LSXy), four reviews and their author responses/followups read. [Decision](https://openreview.net/forum?id=k79Un1LSXy&noteId=bHOF1xpVEs): poster. [Metareview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=k79Un1LSXy&noteId=OGQuT5Rqkk) considers the main concerns resolved for acceptance. MKDX explicitly reports raising 4 to 6 in its [followup](https://openreview.net/forum?id=k79Un1LSXy&noteId=9QlFzBS824).

The decisive clarification is mathematical: [Af2K response part2](https://openreview.net/forum?id=k79Un1LSXy&noteId=UQ4TqX6JSm) and [MKDX part3](https://openreview.net/forum?id=k79Un1LSXy&noteId=g2kDfLPsCL) explicitly disclaim a single globally interpretable KL optimum and a full optimality theory. Their claim is stronger pairwise gradient pressure in pessimistic cases. This supports our graph-integrability concern; a gradient-pressure inequality must not be upgraded to global policy-quality improvement.

Other concerns were stronger-reference confounding, optional home-advantage margin, length-sensitive pessimism, label noise, missing Qwen/configs/AlphaDPO. The authors add [core-only comparisons](https://openreview.net/forum?id=k79Un1LSXy&noteId=u0KcrtADXy), [DPO+chosen-SFT baseline](https://openreview.net/forum?id=k79Un1LSXy&noteId=mllQdexCL7), [FocalPO and SFT-reference comparisons](https://openreview.net/forum?id=k79Un1LSXy&noteId=X5MAe5pKqc), [Qwen/configs](https://openreview.net/forum?id=k79Un1LSXy&noteId=FbyXElxUwW), and [length normalization](https://openreview.net/forum?id=k79Un1LSXy&noteId=bct68Q4s2E). The SFT-reference HyPO remains slightly below SimPO on the quoted two comparisons; stronger references account for part of the full recipe gain. Report these comparisons without claiming every gain is due to clipping.

The reported length correlation .1649 and a HyPO-LN ablation show limited impact in the tested data, not elimination of length bias. Authors discuss noisy-label tradeoffs and robust-method combinations; [Af2K followup](https://openreview.net/forum?id=k79Un1LSXy&noteId=dfbjD8QyrY) still recommends a controlled-noise check, although it is not blocking acceptance. Also, with a fixed offline reference, clipping is constant with respect to policy parameters; a non-smooth reference transformation is not automatically a non-smooth policy objective. Any claim about optimization smoothness must identify which parameters are changing.

## DuPO — dual preference optimization

[Forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=SD8Z231C45), 23/23 replies read. [Decision](https://openreview.net/forum?id=SD8Z231C45&noteId=FqOftbEFzR): poster. [Metareview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=SD8Z231C45&noteId=cQNsiJsfhW) records initial 2/4/6/8, an explicit 2-to-6 change, and recommends acceptance while requesting deeper analysis, controlled comparisons and a clearer statement of scope.

The reviews identify three consequential weaknesses: task-specific decomposition/generalizability; whether inverse consistency measures true task utility; and comparisons against untrained or differently trained models. [tLFR review](https://openreview.net/forum?id=SD8Z231C45&noteId=6xYeKVcukD), [EbeA review](https://openreview.net/forum?id=SD8Z231C45&noteId=77vZk4cWNp), [P36R review](https://openreview.net/forum?id=SD8Z231C45&noteId=HwmYfdE5uI), [S26D review](https://openreview.net/forum?id=SD8Z231C45&noteId=OXpuW5CjTC).

Author replies clarify that the **current policy** produces both primal and inverse answers; the initial model filters dual tasks; math checks the final reconstructed input variable, not the reasoning path; translation uses BLEU. [Implementation response](https://openreview.net/forum?id=SD8Z231C45&noteId=9rqPm63PaM). [Controlled-baseline response](https://openreview.net/forum?id=SD8Z231C45&noteId=mz3hgAd2zi) adds same-data Oracle-RLVR and Oracle-DAPO. [Scope response](https://openreview.net/forum?id=SD8Z231C45&noteId=B9Ad179IpW) acknowledges creative writing remains future work. [tLFR followup](https://openreview.net/forum?id=SD8Z231C45&noteId=8XLa4V3ofj) explicitly raises the score but asks to remove overclaims about open-ended tasks. [P36R followup](https://openreview.net/forum?id=SD8Z231C45&noteId=5HdsFAFMXN) says the figure then showed forward accuracy, not the claimed dual-accuracy curve; authors promise the update.

**Our adjudication:** same-data oracle comparisons improve the empirical case, but similar learning curves do not prove that two reward functions are equally reliable on all outputs. Finite-pool uniqueness is not universal injectivity; a changing inverse model can create correlated errors and reward shortcuts. Those require independent adversarial/collision tests. The source's description of an oracle as a performance upper bound is also too strong: a correct reward is not a guaranteed upper bound on the outcomes of a particular noisy optimizer. Training-mode inference cost and reranking-mode additional cost must be reported separately. No creativity/general open-ended correctness theorem is established by these replies.

## MISP-DPO — importance sampling and multimodal negatives

[Forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=HEFPwoGtTj), 20/20 replies read. [Decision](https://openreview.net/forum?id=HEFPwoGtTj&noteId=WEEeGpUUHc): poster. [Metareview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=HEFPwoGtTj&noteId=7NQxOXTRhj) credits diverse-negative construction, corrected novelty wording and new comparisons; broader capabilities beyond hallucination remain an outstanding evaluation concern.

[HwrY](https://openreview.net/forum?id=HEFPwoGtTj&noteId=mSHirDvqv9) identifies prior listwise LPOI/PLPO, missing OPA-DPO/SymMPO, uncertain finite-sample IS properties and insufficient component/compute ablations. [hiMy](https://openreview.net/forum?id=HEFPwoGtTj&noteId=aWd8XwXCxe) asks for SAE details, negative-count controls, a generative-negative comparison and evaluator reconciliation. [h9st](https://openreview.net/forum?id=HEFPwoGtTj&noteId=Vh8Pu2g5x7) asks for broader tasks and overhead.

Authors add [baselines/novelty clarification](https://openreview.net/forum?id=HEFPwoGtTj&noteId=AMBZsEm9jO), [IS statements](https://openreview.net/forum?id=HEFPwoGtTj&noteId=nHG6LKJwWh), [ablation/cost](https://openreview.net/forum?id=HEFPwoGtTj&noteId=I0n25hwJj2), [SAE/negative controls](https://openreview.net/forum?id=HEFPwoGtTj&noteId=tYXrqy5oJA), [judge clarification](https://openreview.net/forum?id=HEFPwoGtTj&noteId=zX0xfxIYIW), and [SEED-Bench](https://openreview.net/forum?id=HEFPwoGtTj&noteId=Gmso8LXsq7). The source says no weight clipping or self-normalized IS, but does describe greedy top-K offline selection. It reports 33 minutes on one A100 for preprocessing and ~3.6s/sample training versus ~2.6s text DPO/~3.1s mDPO. MMHal absolute baseline shifts are attributed to GPT-4.1-mini versus the earlier evaluator. All these numbers are source-reported.

**Our adjudication:** unbiasedness is valid for an inner sum with exact p/q, full support and draws actually sampled from q, with the full outer prefactor held exact. It is not automatically valid for greedy diversified selection, an estimated normalizer, or the overall clipped update. Bounding the final optimizer gradient does not prove that each pre-clipping gradient difference satisfies the theorem's bound; clipping a random estimator generally changes its expectation. No ESS trace or code reconciliation was independently verified here. SEED-Bench Qwen2.5-VL-7B MISP=.7718 versus DPO=.7724 is a counterexample to blanket dominance. A stronger proposal must distinguish sampling variance, nested-normalizer bias and data-distribution changes instead of claiming generic IS makes all three disappear.

[General response1](https://openreview.net/forum?id=PJdw4VBsXD&noteId=oQxdwSiNG3) justifies exclusion and suggests safe/refusal anchors. It reports **12.4% over-refusal on 250 benign XSTest prompts and 100% judged harmlessness on 200 unsafe prompts**. The final version's unsafe–unsafe exclusion should not be silently attributed to the older local draft. The disputed 2025 metareview is retained as review history, not used to invalidate the 2026 version without checking its actual derivation.

## Uni-DPO — dynamic weights and chosen likelihood

[Forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=G7DBGlgjjp), 32/32 replies read, including four reviews and rebuttals. [Decision](https://openreview.net/forum?id=G7DBGlgjjp&noteId=2AbUKYO5xM): poster. [Metareview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=G7DBGlgjjp&noteId=rZV0TI5B1Y) says the important dispute was additional scalar supervision versus an algorithmic improvement, with self-rewarding results supporting acceptance.

[uYa8 review](https://openreview.net/forum?id=G7DBGlgjjp&noteId=rgMSsbFxMO), [5qbs](https://openreview.net/forum?id=G7DBGlgjjp&noteId=8iJAlkQjA6), [Azer](https://openreview.net/forum?id=G7DBGlgjjp&noteId=jApo14ObVN), [HSvf](https://openreview.net/forum?id=G7DBGlgjjp&noteId=zlNejAFO58) raise scorer transparency/cost, pre-existing reweighting methods, length normalization, focal/NLL novelty, weight miscalibration and stronger baselines. Authors clarify public UltraFeedback scores for v0.1, GPT-4o scalar scores for v0.2, and open/self scorers in ablations; add weighting literature, broader baselines and data details. [Scorer procedure](https://openreview.net/forum?id=G7DBGlgjjp&noteId=EbOLBwwojj), [self-rewarding experiment](https://openreview.net/forum?id=G7DBGlgjjp&noteId=0MfzxnCvy5), [RL comparisons](https://openreview.net/forum?id=G7DBGlgjjp&noteId=rQr6Zs3xE1). The [author IS clarification](https://openreview.net/forum?id=G7DBGlgjjp&noteId=WKiZqqTiel) explicitly says these weights shape useful supervision, rather than recover an on-policy estimator through likelihood ratios.

[uYa8 followup](https://openreview.net/forum?id=G7DBGlgjjp&noteId=AjhsfWEM82) raises 4 to 6 but still requests disclosure that the strongest results use scalar information unavailable to the baselines. [Authors agree](https://openreview.net/forum?id=G7DBGlgjjp&noteId=JbwxcCiKHX). Only one reviewer engaged fully; the metareview's expectations about others raising scores are hypothetical, not observed changes.

**Our adjudication:** self-scoring is evidence against a claim that every gain requires a strong external teacher. It does not identify cardinal reward units, prove reliability under systematic judge bias, or replace an equal-information DPO/SimPO+same-weight baseline. The dynamics weight is a policy-dependent loss component; differentiating through it and treating it as a detached sampling weight give different algorithms. The stronger proposal should address identified targets and error rather than rename another quality/difficulty weighting scheme.

## P²-DPO — perceptual processing and calibration

[Forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ekOwxTn65Y), 36/36 replies loaded. All three reviews, metareview and substantive mechanism, bias, cost and comparison replies read; repeated large numerical/qualitative appendix descriptions are not an independent reproduction. [Decision](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ekOwxTn65Y&noteId=ggPXgsMg7X): poster. [Metareview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ekOwxTn65Y&noteId=6GLeTTI50w) credits Qwen-VL-7B and added comparisons, while acknowledging incompletely resolved limitations.

[m9BD](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ekOwxTn65Y&noteId=uWwlrmllGA) raises self-reinforcement and weak causal evidence; [cpy5](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ekOwxTn65Y&noteId=z4R1ataP1U) identifies Calibrated Self-Rewarding VLMs as a close baseline; [LaWn](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ekOwxTn65Y&noteId=5cabkf8eBx) asks about attention errors, disjoint objects and cost.

The decisive author clarification is that preferences are **constructed once with the frozen initial model**, then used for training, with no updated-policy regeneration loop. [Bias response](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ekOwxTn65Y&noteId=e2TSNnHBg2), [offline pipeline](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ekOwxTn65Y&noteId=PHCO7ps2Ls). Thus “on-policy” describes the initial source relationship, not fresh data from each evolving policy. This paper must not be used as empirical validation of iterative feedback convergence. It reports [~1.85x per-step overhead](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ekOwxTn65Y&noteId=9JIKTKGqw2), with preprocessing and inference costs separate.

[Filter experiment](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ekOwxTn65Y&noteId=nhHEsNDfGW): human-reference/Qwen3-Max criterion flags 510 invalid pairs; PPL+margin removes287, intersection236. That is ~82.2% removal precision but only ~46.3% recall relative to that criterion, leaving274 flagged pairs unremoved. It does not establish that all remaining pairs are valid. [Further clarification](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ekOwxTn65Y&noteId=HB8krLgboR) correctly says CLIPScore compares prompt with images, not generated-answer correctness. [m9BD followup](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ekOwxTn65Y&noteId=BUWGwWNj7D) retains4 because proposed defenses show plausibility rather than a causal demonstration of bias reduction.

Attention reallocation/AFR and inverse-crop controls support a monitoring hypothesis. They do not by themselves prove that visual edits preserve the true semantic answer, or that edit-induced likelihood differences are mutual information. The derived VID/Fisher claims require their stated structural assumptions; the direction of visual learning cannot be inferred from attention mass alone. This suggests controlled intervention validity as a separate future problem, but adding another CLIP weight is already well occupied.

## AW-DPO — reasoning/response safety weights

[5LUh](https://openreview.net/forum?id=OuMNJoKJBQ&noteId=Ll6us2r0RS) and [N5bQ](https://openreview.net/forum?id=OuMNJoKJBQ&noteId=Pf8NsfXMIS) question whether CommonsenseQA head ablation establishes that all safety mechanisms lack reasoning. [uxXE](https://openreview.net/forum?id=OuMNJoKJBQ&noteId=3vlMQER3M4) asks for a same-judge PPO baseline/cost and [mqeq](https://openreview.net/forum?id=OuMNJoKJBQ&noteId=yIlb9YMyvV) requests a deeper weighting theory and adaptive attacks.

These measure repeatability, not agreement with human truth or calibrated score differences. [Pipeline response](https://openreview.net/forum?id=OuMNJoKJBQ&noteId=l8hWlrHBv8) clarifies a prior CoT SFT stage and explicit segmentation tags; [scoring](https://openreview.net/forum?id=OuMNJoKJBQ&noteId=m3kLdxG4SP) uses GPT-4o on full/response/reasoning scenarios. [Same-data response](https://openreview.net/forum?id=OuMNJoKJBQ&noteId=cpDC9Lqrbh) argues DPO on the same LLM-scored data isolates the weighted loss; extra preprocessing remains. [Further experiments](https://openreview.net/forum?id=OuMNJoKJBQ&noteId=TqjVUwW2ra) add Qwen probing and attacks. [Theory response](https://openreview.net/forum?id=OuMNJoKJBQ&noteId=WhIqcfumRT) leaves convergence/stability theory to future work and identifies weight scale alpha as a tuned hyperparameter.

**Our adjudication:** targeted head ablation supports a task-specific intervention result, not universal absence of reasoning in safety. Explicitly formatted rationales are observable text and need not faithfully expose the computation that caused an answer. Judge prompt stability does not prove sign reliability for segment-gap weights, particularly mixed-sign cases. Generic capability retention is not benign false-refusal utility. A future theoretical paper would need an identifiable measurement contract and signed-weight stability conditions; simply adding a second segment weight already collides with this work.

## Is on-policy data always best? — stage-dependent sampling

[Forum](https://openreview.net/forum?id=tz9mJmgrdM). **16/16 public replies visible and read on 13 October.** Four reviews, responses, metareview and decision were available. The resumed browser session is disconnected; the following preserves the findings already read.

- **Decision/context:** [poster decision](https://openreview.net/forum?id=tz9mJmgrdM&noteId=KvP1JR4R6X). The [metareview](https://openreview.net/forum?id=tz9mJmgrdM&noteId=hbEHgMe5Ec) treats the contribution as a useful analytical and empirical diagnostic. Broader model/algorithm coverage and usability were central concerns. Suggested hypothetical score changes in a summary are not actual reviewer edits.
- **Practical boundary estimation:** [review cNBy](https://openreview.net/forum?id=tz9mJmgrdM&noteId=zr5kxv2qNQ) questioned whether the boundary could guide a decision prospectively. The [response](https://openreview.net/forum?id=tz9mJmgrdM&noteId=b7uV9rdjrp) proposes a small preliminary sample and gives 23 minutes of diagnosis, split into nine sampling and fourteen boundary-estimation minutes, versus 16.9 hours end-to-end for an iteration. Its separate 3.2% overhead claim uses a different denominator: 23 minutes divided by 16.9 hours is about 2.27%. Report both denominators explicitly.
- **Scope:** [review NSmT](https://openreview.net/forum?id=tz9mJmgrdM&noteId=jFGNz9BGO8) asked about RLVR and stronger models. The [authors](https://openreview.net/forum?id=tz9mJmgrdM&noteId=zaUvYeVdgi) do not extend the theory to RLVR because its feedback assumptions differ. [Additional Qwen3-4B tests](https://openreview.net/forum?id=tz9mJmgrdM&noteId=HsN1KLyAzD) favor on-policy data in two iterations; they do not establish a universal source ordering.
- **Algorithm/model robustness:** [review j12w](https://openreview.net/forum?id=tz9mJmgrdM&noteId=3Tyn3NMzjS) questions source diversity and generality. Responses add [Qwen experiments](https://openreview.net/forum?id=tz9mJmgrdM&noteId=6x1Li8C7xS) and [SPPO across three models](https://openreview.net/forum?id=tz9mJmgrdM&noteId=Q62QvwdmMC). [Further MT-Bench evidence](https://openreview.net/forum?id=tz9mJmgrdM&noteId=IK3VVmKP6K) responds to narrow evaluation concerns.
- **Mixing is already explored:** [review fQXn](https://openreview.net/forum?id=tz9mJmgrdM&noteId=TFwpX4d5FN) asks about mixed sources and readability. The [mixed-source response](https://openreview.net/forum?id=tz9mJmgrdM&noteId=bIMIEv7FaL) samples one candidate from each source, then labels the cross-source pair. Late-stage Zephyr length-controlled win rates are 25.47 for mixed, 23.77 for off-policy, and 33.28 for on-policy. Generic source mixing and dynamic adaptation cannot be claimed as untouched research ideas.

**Adjudication.** Rank consistency and stage scores are contextual proxies. Agreement on which answer wins does not identify the full preference probability distribution. The [reward–policy bijection response](https://openreview.net/forum?id=tz9mJmgrdM&noteId=OKwLWiJmZe) still needs reward uniqueness only up to a prompt-dependent additive constant; a Boltzmann map also does not make restricted DPO and reward optimization equivalent for a fixed true reward. The cross-source pairing experiment differs from a mixture of complete pair sources: they induce different comparison graphs and weights. These distinctions matter for the new feedback proposal.
